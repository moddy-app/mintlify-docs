AltGuard holds every new human member behind a verification gate until `verify.moddy.app` — a dedicated external service, not the bot itself — decides whether their account looks like a second account of someone already on your server. The bot never sees or stores any personal data from that process: no fingerprint, no email, no IP address. Everything crossing back to the bot is Discord ids, a pass/fail-style verdict, and category labels. See [What AltGuard Checks, and What Happens to That Data](/modules/altguard-data) for exactly what's collected, why, and for how long.

<Warning>
  AltGuard is not a heuristic the bot runs on its own — it relies on an explicit, consent-based check performed by Moddy's verification service in the member's browser. A member must actively go through the verification screen; there is no passive "AI guesses your account age" detection running in the background.
</Warning>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A member joins">
    They immediately receive the **unverified role** and can only see your configured verification channel. Bots skip this entirely — they can't click a button, so they're never gated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They open the verification panel">
    A single permanent message with one button sits in the verification channel. Clicking it opens a consent screen explaining exactly what will be checked (browser characteristics, a technical cookie, Discord email, server list, IP address — all encrypted), with links to the data notice, terms, and privacy policy. Two checkboxes must be ticked to continue; closing the screen sends nothing anywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They authorize on Discord's own screen">
    After consenting, the member gets a personal, single-use link (valid 20 minutes) where the verification service runs its checks. Moddy has no part in this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The verdict comes back">
    The service returns one of three outcomes: **passed** (the member gets the verified role and moves on to Auto Role, if configured), **flagged** (kept behind the gate, pending a moderator's manual review), or **blocked** (kept behind the gate). The member is DMed the outcome in plain language — never the underlying score or signals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AltGuard may occasionally flag a legitimate new member who happens to share signals with a previously banned account. Review flags in your log channel before assuming the worst — that's exactly what the manual `/altguard verify` override below is for.
</Note>

## Enabling AltGuard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the configuration panel">
    Type `/config` in your server and select **AltGuard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a verification channel">
    This becomes the _only_ channel the unverified role can see. Moddy automatically locks every other channel from that role when you save — you don't need to configure channel permissions by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the unverified and verified roles">
    Both are required for the module to activate. The unverified role holds new members back; the verified role is granted once they pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a log channel (recommended)">
    Verdicts and manual staff decisions are posted here. Keep it private to your moderation team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the panel language">
    The verification panel's wording is fixed (every server explains the same data processing to members) — you only choose which of the supported languages it's shown in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Moddy needs the **Manage Roles** and **Manage Channels** permissions, with its own role positioned above both the verified and unverified roles, to lock down channels and hand out roles correctly.
</Warning>

## Reviewing and Overriding Verdicts

Flagged and blocked members stay behind the gate until a moderator steps in. From your log channel, moderators with the **Manage Roles** permission can override any verdict directly in Discord:

```discord
/altguard verify <member>
/altguard unverify <member>
```

`/altguard verify` lets a member through manually — granting the verified role and handing them off to Auto Role, if enabled — while `/altguard unverify` sends a previously verified member back behind the gate.

<Tip>
  If you have questions about a specific decision — why a member was flagged, what score or signals were involved — that detail isn't exposed in `/altguard`. Reach out through the [support server](https://moddy.app/support) with the case's details and the Moddy team can look it up on request.
</Tip>

## Auto Role Integration

If your server also uses the **Auto Role** feature, AltGuard holds it back automatically: a member who hasn't passed the gate gets no auto role, no matter what. The moment they pass — automatically or via a manual `/altguard verify` — Auto Role runs as normal.

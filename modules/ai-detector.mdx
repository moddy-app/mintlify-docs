With AI images everywhere, "is this real?" is a fair question. AI Detector answers it **only when it can prove the answer**: it reads the Content Credentials (C2PA) hidden inside a posted image, checks that the signature comes from a certificate on the [official C2PA trust list](https://c2pa.org), and replies under the image with a short card.

The module is **on by default in every server**. You don't need to set anything up; you can narrow it down or pause it whenever you like.

<Warning>
  **No reply never means "not AI".** Most AI images carry no signature at all, and screenshots, re-saves, and most social networks and messaging apps strip it. AI Detector tells you what a signature *proves*, never what the absence of one *suggests*.
</Warning>

## What Moddy Can Tell You

Under every PNG, JPEG or WebP posted by a member, Moddy replies — without pinging anyone, in your **server language** — only when the image carries a trusted signature or generation metadata:

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Content generated by artificial intelligence** | The signature shows the image was created entirely by an AI tool. The card names the tool and the date when the signature gives them. |
| **Content partially generated by artificial intelligence** | The signed history shows a generative AI tool modified the image. |
| **Authenticated photograph** | A signed camera or phone captured the image, and the history shows no generative AI. |
| **Authenticated and retouched photograph** | Same, with ordinary retouching (crop, colour, resize…) listed on the card. |
| **Content probably generated by artificial intelligence** | An *unsigned* PNG still carries Stable Diffusion or ComfyUI generation parameters. This is a strong hint, not proof — anyone can add or remove those parameters — and the card says so. |

When a message contains several images, Moddy announces the most significant result.

<Note>
  An authenticated photo certifies where the **image** came from, not the truth of the **scene** it shows.
</Note>

## The More Information Button

Every card has a **More information** button. Anyone who reads the card can press it to get a private panel, in their own language, with:

- the result and where it comes from (verified signature or unsigned metadata)
- the signature details: who signed it, the algorithm, the signing time, the tool and the camera
- the signed history of the file, oldest first
- how it works, how far to trust the result, and why a missing result proves nothing

The button keeps working indefinitely, including after Moddy restarts. If the image has since been deleted, the panel says it is no longer available.

## Privacy

Only a short list of fields is ever shown: the signing organisation, the signature algorithm and time, the generator, the camera make and model, and the names, dates and types of editing actions. **GPS position, the author's name, identity information, serial numbers and the certificate name never leave the file.** For PNG metadata, the prompt is never shown — only the generator and its technical settings.

Moddy reads the file as sent by the member and never lets it make Moddy fetch anything from the Internet.

## Configuring AI Detector

Open `/config` and select **AI Detector**.

| Setting | What it controls | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announced results** | Which kinds of results Moddy posts: AI-generated images, AI-edited images, authenticated photos, generation metadata. Untick a kind and Moddy stays silent for it. | All four |
| **Ignored channels** | Channels (and their threads) where Moddy never answers. Up to 25. | None |

Click **Save** to apply your changes. To switch the module off entirely, press **Manage** and set it to **Paused**.

<Tip>
  If you'd rather Moddy only speak up about AI, untick **Authenticated photos** — handy in a photography server where nearly every image is signed.
</Tip>

## `/ai-check`

Not sure about an image that Moddy didn't comment on? Check it yourself.

```discord
/ai-check image:<file>
/ai-check message:<link>
```

<ParamField path="image" type="attachment">
  The image to check, as PNG, JPEG or WebP.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="message" type="string">
  A link to a message **of this server** that contains the image. Moddy only reads it if you can see that channel and its history yourself.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="incognito" type="boolean">
  Whether the reply is visible only to you. Defaults to your `/preferences` setting.
</ParamField>

Unlike the automatic replies, `/ai-check` **always answers**: either the result and its details, or "No verifiable information" with the reason — no signature, an untrusted or invalid one, an unsupported file. It works in servers, in DMs, and as a user-installed command.

## Good to Know

- Moddy checks PNG, JPEG and WebP files up to 25 MB, and at most 4 images per message.
- A result reflects what the signer *declared*. A signature can't be forged without the signer's key, but a tool can sign without saying much, and then Moddy stays silent.
- It isn't certain that every way of uploading to Discord keeps the signature. Moddy reads the original file, which is the best Discord offers.

## Permissions Moddy Needs

Nothing beyond seeing the channel and sending messages in it.

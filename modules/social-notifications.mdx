The Social Notifications module keeps your community informed without anyone having to check external platforms manually. Connect YouTube channels, Twitch streamers, Bluesky accounts, or any RSS feed, and Moddy will post an update in a channel of your choice the moment new content appears. It's the easiest way to keep your server up to date with the creators and topics you care about.

## Setting Up Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the configuration panel">
    Type `/config` in your server to open Moddy's configuration panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Social Notifications">
    Choose **Social Notifications** from the module list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a platform">
    Select the platform you want to follow: YouTube, Twitch, Bluesky, or RSS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account or feed">
    Provide the channel name, username, or feed URL for the account you want to follow. For RSS, paste the full feed URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Discord channel">
    Select the channel in your server where Moddy should post notifications when new content is detected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. Moddy will begin polling that account and notify your chosen channel when new content appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customizing Notifications

Each followed account has its own set of display options, editable from its **Manage** screen in `/config`:

- **Custom message** — text posted alongside the notification card, for a personal touch or extra context (for example, `A new video just dropped! 🎬`).
- **Role mentions** — choose one or more roles to ping every time this specific account posts.
- **Accent color** — set a custom color for the notification card, or leave it to use the platform's default (YouTube red, Twitch purple, etc.).
- **Avatar & media** — toggle whether the account's profile picture and a large media preview (thumbnail, video embed) are shown on the card.

These settings are per-followed-account, so you can, for example, ping `@Streamers` for one Twitch channel and keep another silent.

## Free vs Moddy Max

The speed at which Moddy checks for new content, and how many accounts you can follow per platform, depend on your plan:

| Platform | Free polling | Moddy Max polling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | every 300s (5 min) | every 60s (1 min) |
| Twitch | every 120s (2 min) | every 30s |
| RSS | every 600s (10 min) | every 120s (2 min) |
| Bluesky | realtime | realtime |

Accounts per platform: **1** on the free plan, **5** on [Moddy Max](/premium/overview) — this applies per platform, so a free server could follow 1 YouTube channel, 1 Twitch streamer, 1 Bluesky account, and 1 RSS feed at the same time.

<Note>
  When several servers follow the same account, Moddy always polls at the fastest interval any of them qualifies for — a free server benefits from a premium server's faster polling on a shared target, at no extra cost to either.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Keep your notification posts organized by using a dedicated channel like `#announcements` or `#content-feed`. This prevents social updates from getting lost in general chat and makes it easy for members to find the content they're interested in.
</Tip>

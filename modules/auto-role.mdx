Auto Role hands out roles automatically to anyone who joins. It keeps **two separate lists** — one for human members, one for bots — so an invited bot can land straight in a "Bots" role without picking up your member roles.

Typical uses: a baseline "Member" role that unlocks the server, a default colour role, or a role that pings a category of content.

## Setting It Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the configuration panel">
    Type `/config` in your server and select **Auto Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the member roles">
    Choose the roles every new human member should receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the bot roles (optional)">
    Choose the roles bots should receive when they're added to the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Auto Role activates as soon as at least one role is set in either list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it controls | Default | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Member roles** | Roles given to human members on join | None | Up to 25 roles |
| **Bot roles** | Roles given to bots on join | None | Up to 25 roles |

The module is active as soon as **either** list has at least one role. Saving with both lists empty is refused.

## Permissions and Role Hierarchy

<Warning>
  Moddy needs the **Manage Roles** permission, and **Moddy's own role must sit above every role you select** in your server's role list. Saving is refused otherwise, and if a role is later moved above Moddy, it's silently skipped on join.
</Warning>

Three kinds of role can never be selected:

- `@everyone`
- **Managed roles** — those created and owned by an integration, such as a bot's own role or the Server Booster role. Discord doesn't allow anyone to assign these.
- Roles that no longer exist

## Working With AltGuard

<Note>
  If [AltGuard](/modules/altguard) is enabled on your server, Auto Role **waits**. A human member who hasn't passed the verification gate receives no auto roles — otherwise the gate would be pointless. The roles are granted the moment they pass, automatically or via a manual `/altguard verify`.

  Bots are never gated, so bot roles apply immediately. And if AltGuard can't be reached at all, Auto Role fails safe: it grants nothing rather than risk letting an unverified member through.
</Note>

## Restoring Roles Instead

Auto Role gives the _same_ roles to _everyone_. If you want returning members to get back the specific roles they had before they left, that's a different module — see [Auto-Restore Roles](/modules/auto-restore-roles). The two work together: a returning member can receive their restored roles _and_ your auto roles.

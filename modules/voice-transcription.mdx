Voice Transcription turns voice messages into readable text, so people who can't listen — in a meeting, on mute, hard of hearing, or just in a noisy room — still get the content.

## Two Ways to Use It

|  | **Transcribe** context menu | **The in-server button** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it works | Everywhere — servers, DMs, user-installed contexts | Only servers with this module enabled |
| Needs the module? | **No** — always available to anyone | **Yes** |
| Who can trigger it | The person who opens the menu | Anyone who can see the message |
| Result | Posted **publicly** in the channel | Posted **publicly**, replacing the button |

<Note>
  The **Transcribe** right-click menu works for everyone, everywhere, regardless of this module. What the module adds is the convenience of a button sitting under every voice message in the channels you choose — or fully automatic transcription with no click at all.
</Note>

Transcriptions are always **public**: the whole point is that the channel can read the voice note. Only failures are private, and only to the person who asked.

## Setting It Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the configuration panel">
    Type `/config` in your server and select **Voice Transcription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the module on">
    Unlike most modules, this one has a real on/off switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode">
    **Button** posts a Transcribe button under each voice message. **Automatic** transcribes every voice message with no click required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restrict to channels (optional)">
    Leave the channel list empty to cover every channel, or pick specific ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it controls | Default | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Status** | Module on/off | Off until configured | — |
| **Mode** | Button, or fully automatic | Button | One of the two |
| **Channels** | Restrict to specific channels | Empty = **every channel** | Up to 25 channels |

Channels can be text channels, voice channels, or threads (public and private).

<Tip>
  **Automatic** mode is best in a dedicated channel where voice notes are the norm and everyone benefits from a transcript. In a general chat, **Button** mode is less noisy — the transcription only appears when someone actually needs it.
</Tip>

## What Can Be Transcribed

- **Discord voice messages**, and any audio attachment
- Formats: `.ogg`, `.oga`, `.opus`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.webm`, `.flac`, `.aac`, `.mpga`, `.mpeg`
- **Maximum file size: 25 MB**
- **Maximum duration: 30 minutes**

Transcriptions longer than 3,500 characters are shown as a preview with the full text attached as a `transcription.txt` file.

The finished card shows the transcription, the detected language, the audio's duration, and who requested it. Nothing in a transcription can ping anyone.

## Permissions

Moddy needs **Send Messages** in the channels you're covering — checked when you save and again at runtime, so Moddy stays silent where it can't speak — plus **View Channel** and **Read Message History** to fetch the audio.

## Things to Know

- **Bots' voice messages are ignored.**
- **In Automatic mode, failures are silent.** If a transcription can't be produced, the loading placeholder disappears and nothing is posted — there's no error message in the channel. In Button mode, the person who clicked gets a private explanation and the button comes back.
- **Two people clicking the same button pay for the audio once** — the second click joins the first request rather than starting a new one.
- If Moddy is being heavily used across all servers, a transcription can be briefly rate-limited, and the person who asked is told how long to wait.

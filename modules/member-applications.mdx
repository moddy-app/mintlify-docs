Discord lets a server ask for an application before anyone can join: new members fill in a short form — accept the rules, answer a few questions — and wait for your team to let them in. This is Discord's **Apply to Join** feature (also called *Server Member Applications*).

The form and the gate belong to Discord. Member Applications takes care of the **review**: every submitted application lands in a channel of your choice as a clear card, with **Approve** and **Reject** buttons your team can use without leaving the conversation.

<Note>
  Moddy doesn't create or edit your form. You turn on **Apply to Join** and write the questions in your Discord server settings. The `/config` panel tells you whether applications are enabled on your server — if they aren't, Moddy has nothing to review.
</Note>

## Setting It Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Apply to Join in Discord">
    In your server settings, enable **Apply to Join** and write the questions you want applicants to answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the configuration panel">
    Type `/config` in your server and select **Member Applications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a review channel">
    This is where every new application is posted. Keep it private to your staff: the card shows the applicant's answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who gets notified and who may decide (optional)">
    Add roles to mention when an application arrives, reviewer roles, and preset rejection reasons — see [Settings](#settings).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The module is active as soon as a review channel is set. There's no separate on/off switch: to stop using it, delete its configuration from the panel.

## The Application Card

Each application is posted once, as a card written in your server's language (set in `/config` → _Server settings_):

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Applicant** | Their mention, display name, username, ID, when their account was created (flagged as *recent account* when it's less than a week old), and how many times they applied before — and how many of those were rejected |
| **Answers** | Every question of your form with the applicant's answer, including whether they accepted the server rules |
| **Status** | *Awaiting review*, then who decided, when, and — for a rejection — the reason |

The roles you chose are mentioned above the card **only when it's first posted** — never again when it updates.

## Approving and Rejecting

- **Approve** lets the applicant in, in one click.
- **Reject** opens a short form: pick one of your preset reasons, write your own (up to 160 characters — it wins over the preset), or leave both empty to reject without a reason.

<Warning>
  Discord shows the rejection reason **to the applicant**. Write it as you would want them to read it.
</Warning>

Once a decision is taken, the buttons disappear and the card shows the outcome. The card stays up to date in every case:

- **Decided in Discord's own review screen** — the card closes and shows who decided there.
- **Withdrawn by the applicant** — the card is marked *Withdrawn by the applicant*.
- **Two moderators click at the same time** — the first decision wins; the second one is told the application was already handled.

## Who Can Decide

Anyone with the **Kick Members** permission can approve or reject — that's the permission Discord itself requires to handle applications.

**Reviewer roles** let you hand the decision to a recruitment team **without** giving it Kick Members. Moddy acts with its own permission on their behalf, and only for applications.

## Settings

| Setting | What it controls | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review channel** | Where application cards are posted. Required. | One text channel |
| **Roles to notify** | Mentioned when a new application arrives | Up to 5 |
| **Reviewer roles** | Can approve or reject, in addition to members with Kick Members | Up to 10 |
| **Preset rejection reasons** | Offered in one click when rejecting. One reason per line. | Up to 10, 100 characters each |

## Permissions

Moddy needs the **Kick Members** permission: Discord requires it to read and handle applications. It also needs to **view and send messages** in the review channel — this is checked when you save.

If Moddy loses Kick Members later, clicking a button tells you so instead of failing silently.

## Good to Know

- **A new application usually appears within a couple of minutes.** Moddy checks your server regularly, so nothing gets lost even if an event is missed.
- **Moddy can't interview applicants.** Discord's interview is a private group chat that bots cannot join. Talk to them from Discord's review screen if you need to.
- **Applications are kept for 180 days**, like Discord keeps them, then deleted.
- **Each application gets exactly one card**, even if Moddy restarts while it arrives.

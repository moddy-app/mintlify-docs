Moddy is ready to use the moment you add it to your server. This page walks you through adding the bot, logging into the dashboard, enabling your first module, and bringing your moderation team on board.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Moddy to Your Server">
    Open the [Moddy page on the Discord App Directory](https://discord.com/application-directory/1375596170713239622) and click **Add to Server**. Select the server you want to add Moddy to, then confirm the requested permissions.

    <Note>
      Moddy works best with the **Administrator** permission. If you prefer a more restricted setup, grant at minimum: **Manage Messages**, **Manage Roles**, **Ban Members**, **Kick Members**, and **Moderate Members**. Missing permissions may prevent certain features from working correctly.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Head to [dashboard.moddy.app](https://dashboard.moddy.app) and click **Log in with Discord**. After authenticating, select the server you just added Moddy to. You'll land on your server's overview page where all configuration options are available.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your First Module">
    From the dashboard, navigate to the **Modules** section and enable **Welcome Messages** to greet new members or turn on **AutoMod** to start filtering harmful content with AI-powered moderation.

    Prefer staying in Discord? Type `/config` in any server channel to open a native configuration panel and enable or adjust modules without leaving the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Share Moddy with your moderators so they can manage cases and run moderation commands. Team members need the **Manage Server** permission in Discord to access `/config` and server-level settings. Moderators with appropriate role permissions can also browse and manage cases using `/cases` and `/mod case`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Moddy is under active development. New features, modules, and improvements ship regularly. Check the [support server](https://moddy.app/support) for the latest announcements and to share feedback with the team.
</Note>

Moddy's utility commands are **globally available**: you can use them in your DMs with Moddy, in any server you share with Moddy, or even in servers that haven't added Moddy. They're standalone tools that need no server setup and no permissions.

<Note>
  **Almost every command takes an `incognito` option.** Leave it blank and Moddy uses your saved preference from `/preferences` — which defaults to **private**. Set it explicitly to override for one command.
</Note>

---

## `/translate`

Translate text into another language. Moddy always auto-detects the source language.

<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>
  The text to translate. Up to 3000 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="to" type="choice">
  The target language, from a fixed list of 25. If omitted, Moddy translates into your Discord client's own language.
</ParamField>

**Available languages:** English (US), English (UK), French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Portuguese (BR), Dutch, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Turkish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Greek, Czech, Romanian, Hungarian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian.

```text
/translate text:Bonjour tout le monde to:🇺🇸 English (US)
/translate text:Hello, how are you?
```

Limited to 20 uses per minute per user. There is no source-language option — DeepL detects it.

## `/fix`, `/rephrase`, `/summarize`

AI writing helpers. All three take up to **2000 characters** in, return up to 3500 out, and are limited to **10 uses per minute per user**.

<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>
  The text to process.
</ParamField>

| Command | Extra option | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/fix` | — | Fixes spelling, grammar, punctuation, and accents — nothing else |
| `/rephrase` | `style` | Neutral _(default)_, Professional, Formal, Friendly, Casual, Concise |
| `/summarize` | `length` | Short, Medium _(default)_, Bullet points |

```text
/fix text:teh quikc brown fox
/rephrase text:Hey can u send that file style:Professional
/summarize text:<a long paragraph> length:Bullet points
```

<Tip>
  You don't have to paste text in. Right-click any message, open **Apps**, and choose **AI text tools** to run any of the three on it directly.
</Tip>

## `/reminder-add`

Schedule a reminder Moddy will send you when the time comes.

<ParamField path="message" type="string" required>
  What to remind you about. Up to 1000 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="time" type="string" required>
  When — relative durations like `1h30m`, `2d`, or natural phrasing like `tomorrow 3pm`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="send_here" type="boolean">
  Post the reminder in the current channel instead of DMing you. Defaults to `false`.
</ParamField>

```text
/reminder-add message:Check the server for new applications time:2h
/reminder-add message:Stream starts! time:1h send_here:true
```

You can have up to **50** active reminders. Times are interpreted in your own timezone, auto-detected from your Discord language or set in `/preferences`.

## `/reminders`

Opens a panel listing your pending reminders, so you can review or delete them.

---

## `/library`

Opens your personal saved-messages library, 10 per page. You save a message by right-clicking it and choosing **Save Message** from the Apps menu — up to **500** saved messages.

---

## `/roll`

Picks a random whole number.

<ParamField path="max" type="integer">
  The highest possible value. Defaults to `6`, like a standard die. Values above 1,000,000 are capped.
</ParamField>

```text
/roll
/roll max:20
```

<Note>
  `/roll` does **not** support dice notation — `2d6` or `1d20+5` won't work. Use the numeric `max` option instead.
</Note>

---

## `/preferences`

Opens your personal settings panel. This is where you set your **default privacy** (whether command replies are private unless you say otherwise) and your **timezone**, which reminders use.

---

## `/subscription`

Shows your [Moddy Max](/premium/overview) status: your tier, expiry date, whether billing is linked, which servers your subscription covers, and a button to manage it.

---

## `/ping`

Checks Moddy's latency and status. Useful for confirming whether the bot is responding slowly.

---

## `/moddy`

General information about the bot — links to the website, the support server, and these docs.

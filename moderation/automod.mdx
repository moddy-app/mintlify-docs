Moddy's AutoMod uses artificial intelligence to monitor messages in your server and take action automatically when harmful content is detected. Instead of relying solely on keyword lists, it understands context — catching subtle violations that simple filters miss, and avoiding false positives on legitimate messages.

## How It Works

Every message sent in a channel where AutoMod is active is evaluated through a series of checks, from fastest to most thorough:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quick pre-filter">
    Very short messages and other content that is trivially safe are cleared instantly without any further processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Safe phrase check">
    Common, well-known safe phrases are matched against an allowlist and passed through immediately, keeping detection fast for normal conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Harmful pattern matching">
    The message is compared against known harmful patterns. Anything that clearly matches a blocked pattern is flagged for action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI evaluation">
    Messages that make it through the earlier checks are evaluated by an AI model. **The AI model makes the final decision** — it takes action only when it is confident the message is harmful, which minimises false positives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action applied">
    If the AI flags the message, it is deleted immediately. A sanction (warn, mute, or ban) is applied to the member based on the severity of the violation and your server's configured AutoMod settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling AutoMod

You can enable AutoMod from the web dashboard or directly inside Discord using `/config`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open server configuration">
    Run `/config` in your server, or visit the [Moddy dashboard](https://dashboard.moddy.app) and select your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to AutoMod AI">
    Find the **AutoMod AI** section in the configuration panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the alert channel">
    Pick a text channel to receive AutoMod's alert cards. This channel is **required** — AutoMod won't run without one set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle AutoMod on">
    Enable the toggle to activate AutoMod for your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust severity">
    Pick a severity level from 1 to 5. This controls how sensitive detection is and how harshly the automatic sanction scale escalates — it's the only manual dial; Moddy decides the specific action (warn, mute, or ban) and duration for each violation on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exempt roles or channels (optional)">
    Add trusted roles or channels to the exemption lists if you want AutoMod to skip them entirely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review the AutoMod logs regularly in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.moddy.app) to see what messages are being caught and flagged. This helps you tune the sensitivity so AutoMod works well for your community's tone and style.
</Tip>

## Actions AutoMod Can Take

When AutoMod determines a message is harmful, it deletes the message and applies one of the following sanctions, chosen automatically from the violation's category, severity, and the member's history — there's no manual mapping to configure:

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Warn** | A formal warning is recorded in the member's case. Suitable for minor or first-time violations. |
| **Mute** | The member is temporarily timed out. You can configure the mute duration in your AutoMod settings. |
| **Ban** | The member is banned from the server. Reserved for the most severe violations. |

All AutoMod actions are recorded as moderation cases with a full timeline, just like manually-applied sanctions.

## Getting Smarter Over Time

AutoMod doesn't just react to a single message in isolation — two feedback loops shape future decisions for your server, with no manual tuning required:

- **Appeal outcomes.** An accepted appeal doesn't just undo the sanction — it's a signal that the original call was a false positive, so it stops counting against that member for future escalation decisions. A refused appeal, on the other hand, confirms the sanction was right and weighs slightly more than an unreviewed one.
- **Server precedents.** As your moderators accept or refuse appeals, Moddy builds a memory of what your specific community considers sanctionable — so its judgment calls become more consistent with how your team actually moderates, rather than relying on generic rules alone.

## Appealing AutoMod Decisions

When AutoMod sanctions a member, Moddy sends them a **direct message** immediately — explaining what happened and including an **Appeal** button. The member can submit an appeal without leaving Discord.

Submitted appeals are routed to your server's moderators for review. Moderators can:

- **Accept** the appeal — the sanction is revoked.
- **Refuse** the appeal — the sanction stands.
- **Transform** the appeal — replace the sanction with a different one.

Every appeal outcome is logged in the case timeline.

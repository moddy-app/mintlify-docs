A sanction is a disciplinary action applied to a member as part of a moderation case. Moddy supports several sanction types — from a formal warning all the way to a ban — and records every one of them in the case timeline so your moderation history is always complete and auditable.

## Sanction Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Warn" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A formal warning issued to a member. Warns are recorded in the case timeline but carry no automatic Discord-level consequence. They serve as an official, auditable notice to the member.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mute" icon="microphone-slash">
    A temporary timeout that prevents the member from sending messages or joining voice channels. You can configure an expiry date — when it lapses, the mute is lifted automatically and the case is updated.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ban" icon="ban">
    Removes the member from the server and prevents them from rejoining. Bans are recorded in the case timeline and can be revoked through Moddy to lift the Discord ban simultaneously.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Kicks are not recorded as cases.** Because a kick doesn't leave a lasting Discord-level consequence, Moddy does not create or update a case when a member is kicked — whether through Moddy commands or directly via Discord.
</Note>

<Info>
  These three are **server-scoped** sanctions, issued and managed entirely through `/cases`. They're separate from Moddy's own **global sanctions** — _Global warning_, _Limited_, and _Suspended_ — which the Moddy team applies platform-wide against accounts or servers that break Moddy's own terms of service, independently of any single server's moderation.
</Info>

## Applying Sanctions

Open the `/cases` panel and start a sanction against a member:

```discord
/cases
```

- If the member has no open case in your server, adding a sanction creates one — you pick the sanction type, an optional duration (for mutes), and a reason, and the case and its first sanction are created together.
- If the member already has an open case, adding a sanction appends to it instead of opening a duplicate — useful for escalating, like upgrading a warn to a mute after repeated behaviour.

Each action button is gated by the matching Discord permission: **Timeout Members** to add a warn or mute, **Ban Members** to add a ban.

Sanctions are also applied **automatically by AutoMod** when harmful messages are detected. AutoMod-created sanctions appear in the case timeline just like manually-applied ones.

## Revoking Sanctions

Open the case from `/cases` and use the **Revoke** action on the active sanction — it requires the same permission as the sanction being revoked (Timeout Members for a warn/mute, Ban Members for a ban).

Revoking a sanction removes the Discord-level consequence immediately — a mute is lifted, a ban is removed — and updates the case timeline with a revocation entry. If revoking that sanction means there are no remaining active sanctions on the case, Moddy closes the case automatically.

<Tip>
  If you accept a member's appeal, Moddy handles the revocation automatically — you don't need to run `/mod case revoke` separately.
</Tip>

## Auto-Recorded Sanctions

Moddy watches for bans and timeouts applied directly through Discord — without using any Moddy commands — and records them as cases automatically, using the audit log entry to capture the real moderator and reason. Unbanning a member or clearing a timeout through Discord likewise revokes the matching sanction, so the case can auto-close on its own. Actions taken by other bots are ignored.

<Warning>
  Auto-recording requires Moddy to have the **View Audit Log** permission in your server. Without it, bans and timeouts applied outside of `/cases` won't be picked up.
</Warning>

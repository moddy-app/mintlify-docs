This Privacy Policy describes Moddy's practices regarding the collection, use, storage, and protection of personal data, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection laws.

  **Last updated: December 29, 2025.**
</Info>

## Data Controller

**Moddy.** For any request or question, see the [Contact](#contact) section at the end of this document.

## Personal Data Collected

### Automatically collected data

When using our services, we automatically collect:

- **Discord identifiers**: user ID, server ID, channel ID
- **Discord profile information**: username, server name, avatars
- **Logging data**: error logs, technical information, command execution context
- **Usage data**: interactions with the bot, executed commands, system events
- **API call telemetry**: for features that call an external AI provider, we log the provider, model, token counts, cost, and the associated server and user ID

<Note>
  Moddy itself never collects your email address. The only flow involving an email is the [AltGuard verification](/modules/altguard-data), where your Discord email address is transmitted **to the external verification service** — never to the bot — and only after you explicitly consent.
</Note>

### Data collected when you use a feature

Some features store data only when you actively use them:

| Data | What is stored | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Saved messages** | The full raw message data, including content, metadata, and attachment URLs | 500 per user |
| **Custom reminders** | Content, date, time, and associated identifiers | 50 per user |
| **Inter-server messages** | Content, author, origin server, broadcast status | — |
| **Saved Discord roles** | List of role identifiers (if the module is enabled) | — |
| **Moderation cases** | Sanctions, evidence, reasons, notes, complete history | — |
| **Sanction appeals** | The appeal reason you write, its route and status | — |
| **Social notification subscriptions** | Followed accounts, custom message templates, the ID of who added them | — |
| **AltGuard verification records** | The verdict, score, and reasons returned by the verification service, plus your gate status in that server | — |

### Data collected by automated moderation

<Warning>
  Unlike the features above, AI automatic moderation operates **without a per-message action on your part**. Where it is enabled by a server, message content may be read and stored as described below. It runs only in servers, never in DMs.
</Warning>

When AI automatic moderation is enabled on a server:

- **Message content is analysed.** Text that the detection funnel escalates is sent to OpenAI for embedding and classification.
- **Sanctioned messages are stored as evidence** on the resulting moderation case, along with a snapshot of the surrounding channel context (up to 15 messages, which may include messages from other members), the author's name, and a jump link.
- **A quality-evaluation corpus** may retain the message content, its context, and the verdict, so detection accuracy can be measured and improved.
- **Server precedents** retain a normalised copy of moderated message text and its embedding, per server, so future decisions stay consistent with how your moderators actually rule. This store is capped at 500 entries per server, with the oldest evicted.

Separately, **Discord token leak detection** scans messages for leaked Discord tokens. When one is found, the token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) alongside a masked copy of the message and the author, server, and channel names, so the owner can be alerted and the token invalidated.

### Configuration and preferences data

- **User attributes**: statuses such as BETA, PREMIUM, DEVELOPER, and identity attributes such as TEAM or verified-organisation markers
- **Custom configuration**: usage preferences, bot settings
- **Server configuration**: enabled modules, configured channels, custom prefixes, and bot customization (per-server nickname, bio, avatar and banner references)

<Note>
  Platform-level sanctions issued by the Moddy team are **no longer stored as a user attribute**. They are recorded as ordinary moderation cases at one of three levels — global warning, limited, or suspended — and may target a user or a whole server. This replaces the legacy "BLACKLISTED" status described in earlier versions of this policy.
</Note>

### Payment data

Through our payment provider Stripe, we process payment information (tokenized card number, cardholder name, billing address), transaction history (amounts, dates, statuses), and Stripe customer and transaction references.

<Warning>
  Sensitive payment information (full card numbers, CVV) is **never** stored in our infrastructure. It is processed and secured directly by Stripe.
</Warning>

### Cookies and navigation data (website)

Our website may use technical cookies necessary for functionality, analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to measure audience, and preference cookies remembering your choices. You can configure your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

## Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed for the following purposes:

- **Service provision**: command execution, feature management, moderation
- **Account management**: authentication, user preferences, subscriptions
- **Payment processing**: transaction management, billing, fraud prevention
- **Customer support**: technical assistance, problem resolution, request handling
- **Service improvement**: performance analysis, error correction, moderation accuracy, new feature development
- **Security**: abuse prevention, suspicious activity detection, leaked credential alerts, rule enforcement
- **Legal obligations**: response to authority requests, regulatory compliance
- **Communications**: essential notifications, service updates

### Important warning about external services

<Warning>
  **Data transmitted to content-processing services is not anonymized.** This applies to OpenAI, DeepL, Groq, and any comparable service.

  Discord identifiers may be anonymized or replaced with opaque references during transmission, but **the content you submit — message text, translations, queries, and voice recordings — is transmitted as is**.

  **Some data sent to the OpenAI API may be used by OpenAI to train and improve its models**, in accordance with their terms of use.

  Never include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in your queries to translation, AI, transcription, or content-processing services. This includes, but is not limited to: passwords, banking information, medical information, and confidential professional or personal documents.
</Warning>

## Data Retention Period

**Collected personal data is retained indefinitely** until either manual deletion performed by our technical team, or an explicit deletion request from you as part of exercising your right to erasure.

The following are exceptions with a **shorter, automatic** retention:

- **Leaked token records** — the encrypted token is purged after 24 hours, or immediately once invalidated
- **Server precedents** used by automatic moderation — capped at 500 entries per server, oldest evicted first
- **AltGuard verification data** — held by the external verification service on its own schedule, detailed in the [AltGuard data notice](/modules/altguard-data) (90 days to 18 months depending on the signal, 3 years for the proof of consent)

## Security and Data Confidentiality

### Technical security measures

- **Encryption in transit**: all communications between our services use HTTPS/TLS
- **Encryption at rest**: data encrypted in our databases according to Railway standards; leaked tokens are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- **No local file storage**: files are never written to disk on our servers. Audio submitted for transcription transits our process in memory only and is streamed directly to the transcription provider; for saved messages and evidence, only URLs hosted by Discord's CDN are retained.

### Access control

- **Database access**: reserved exclusively for Moddy developers and managers
- **Support team**: does not have direct database access, but may consult certain information as part of customer support
- **Stripe data**: our team has no access to the complete payment information stored by Stripe

### Non-commercialization commitment

<Note>
  **Moddy never sells your personal data to third parties.** Your information is only shared with the service providers necessary for our platform's operation, as listed in [Data Sharing with Third-Party Providers](#data-sharing-with-third-party-providers).
</Note>

### Security incident notification

In case of a personal data breach likely to pose a risk to your rights and freedoms, we commit to:

- Notify the competent supervisory authority (CNIL in France) within **72 hours** of discovering the incident
- Inform you as soon as possible by email or via our Discord server
- Communicate the nature of the breach, the measures taken, and recommendations to mitigate risks

## Data Not Collected

Moddy **never** collects:

- Personal passwords (except Discord authentication)
- Precise GPS geolocation data
- Web browsing history
- Address books or personal contacts
- Biometric data
- The content of your private messages, except where you explicitly save a message yourself

<Warning>
  **One exception on IP addresses.** Moddy the bot does not collect or store IP addresses. However, if a server you join uses **AltGuard**, the external verification service processes your IP address as part of the check — never in clear text, only as a non-reversible fingerprint, and only after you explicitly consent on the verification screen. The bot never receives it. See the [AltGuard data notice](/modules/altguard-data) for the full detail.
</Warning>

## Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of access**: obtain confirmation that your data is being processed and receive a copy
- **Right to rectification**: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to erasure** (right to be forgotten): obtain deletion of your personal data
- **Right to restriction of processing**: request processing restriction under certain circumstances
- **Right to data portability**: receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
- **Right to object**: object to the processing of your data for reasons relating to your particular situation
- **Right to withdraw consent**: where processing is based on your consent
- **Right to lodge a complaint**: file a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (CNIL in France)

We commit to responding to any request within a maximum period of **one month** from its receipt. This period may be extended by two additional months for a complex request, in which case you will be informed of the extension.

As part of providing our services, your personal data may be transferred and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), particularly the United States.

These transfers are made to the providers listed in [Data Sharing with Third-Party Providers](#data-sharing-with-third-party-providers).

We ensure that these transfers are made in accordance with appropriate legal mechanisms provided by the GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) approved by the European Commission, adequacy decisions for certain countries, and provider-specific appropriate safeguards.

## Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your personal data is based on the following legal grounds:

- **Contract performance**: provision of requested services (Article 6.1.b GDPR)
- **Consent**: where you explicitly accept certain processing, such as AltGuard verification (Article 6.1.a GDPR)
- **Legitimate interest**: service improvement, security, fraud prevention (Article 6.1.f GDPR)
- **Legal obligation**: regulatory compliance, responses to authorities (Article 6.1.c GDPR)

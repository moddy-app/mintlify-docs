## Service Description

### Services offered

Moddy offers the following features:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Moderation">
    - **AI automatic moderation** — analyses messages through an AI pipeline and applies deletions, warnings, mutes, or bans according to a deterministic sanction scale, with recorded evidence and a binding appeal process
    - **Manual moderation commands** — apply warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans to one or more members
    - **Moderation history** — every sanction is recorded as a structured case with a reference code and full timeline, browsable via `/cases` (server scope) and `/mycases` (your own cases across all servers)
    - **AltGuard** — an anti multi-account verification gate that holds joining members behind an external verification check before they can access the server
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Server management and automation">
    - **Welcome messages** — up to 5 configurable welcome cards in channels, plus welcome DMs to new members
    - **Role management** — automatic role assignment on join, and role restoration when a member rejoins
    - **Starboard** — reaction-based highlighting of popular messages in a dedicated channel
    - **Adaptive slowmode** — automatic channel slowmode adjusted to live activity
    - **Social notifications** — posts a server notification when followed YouTube, Twitch, Bluesky, or RSS accounts publish
    - **Cross-server messages** — message relay between linked channels of different Discord servers
    - **Bot customization** — a per-server nickname, avatar, banner, and bio (Moddy Max), and display-name style (free)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Utilities and artificial intelligence">
    - **Reminder system** — scheduled personalized notifications
    - **AI text tools** — `/fix`, `/rephrase`, and `/summarize`, powered by OpenAI
    - **Translation** — `/translate`, powered by DeepL
    - **Voice message transcription** — converts voice messages to text on demand or automatically
    - **Message bookmarking** — save messages privately for later retrieval
    - **Utility and informational commands** — various tools for server management and lookups
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Security">
    - **Discord token leak detection** — detects Discord tokens posted in messages, validates them, and alerts the owner so they can be invalidated. Detected tokens are encrypted at rest and purged after 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Moddy Max is Moddy's premium subscription. It unlocks a focused set of extra features — full bot customization and faster, higher-capacity social notifications — on up to 5 of your Discord servers. Core features like moderation cases, AutoMod AI, welcome messages, and utility commands are the same on every server, free or premium.

## Getting Moddy Max

Subscribe to Moddy Max at [moddy.app](https://moddy.app) to get started. Once subscribed, head to the [Premium Servers page](https://dashboard.moddy.app/select-premium-servers) on your dashboard to choose which of your servers should receive premium benefits. You can update your server selection at any time.

## Activating Premium on a Server

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to Moddy Max">
    Visit [moddy.app](https://moddy.app) and complete your subscription to Moddy Max.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Go to [dashboard.moddy.app](https://dashboard.moddy.app) and sign in with your Discord account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Premium Servers">
    Find the **Premium Servers** section in your dashboard to manage which servers receive premium features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Servers">
    Choose up to 5 servers from the list of servers where you have Moddy installed. You can change your selection at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Selection">
    Save your changes — premium is activated on your selected servers immediately. No restart or re-invite required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your Moddy Max subscription is tied to your Discord account. A server stays premium as long as _at least one_ subscriber who selected it still has an active subscription — so if you cancel, every server only you had selected loses premium once the subscription lapses. Use `/subscription` in Discord at any time to check your current plan and renewal date.
</Note>

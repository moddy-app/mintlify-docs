Upgrading to Moddy Max enhances several key areas of the bot: AutoMod AI gets faster with a higher quota, social notifications become near real-time, and you gain the ability to fully customize Moddy's appearance on your server. Core features like moderation cases, welcome messages, and utility commands remain available on every server regardless of plan.

## Feature Comparison

| Feature | Free | Moddy Max |
|---|---|---|
| Servers with premium features | 0 | Up to 5 |
| AutoMod AI | ✓ Standard speed | ✓ Faster, higher quota |
| Social Notifications — accounts per platform | Limited | More accounts |
| Social Notifications — polling speed | Standard | Near real-time |
| Bot Customization (nickname, avatar, banner, bio) | ✗ | ✓ |
| Premium badge | ✗ | ✓ |
| VIP support | ✗ | ✓ (skip the queue) |
| Welcome Messages | ✓ Up to 5 channels | ✓ Up to 5 channels |
| Moderation Cases | ✓ | ✓ |
| Utility commands | ✓ | ✓ |

## Bot Customization

With Moddy Max, you can change Moddy's nickname, avatar, banner, and bio on each of your premium servers, making the bot feel native to your community's brand. Whether you want Moddy to blend in with a custom name or stand out with a unique avatar, full customization gives you complete control over how the bot presents itself to your members.

## VIP Support

Moddy Max subscribers get priority access to the Moddy support team. Join the support Discord at [moddy.app/support](https://moddy.app/support) — your VIP role will be applied automatically once your subscription is detected. With VIP access, your support requests are handled ahead of the general queue so you can get back to running your server faster.

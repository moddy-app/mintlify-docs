## How Moddy Fits Together

Moddy is organized around three main building blocks:

- **Modules** are per-server features you turn on and configure individually — Welcome Messages, Social Notifications, Inter-Server bridging, AltGuard, and AutoMod AI. Nothing runs until you enable it.
- **Moderation cases** are the audit trail: every warning, mute, kick, or ban — whether issued through Moddy or done natively in Discord — is recorded as a structured case with a reference code, a timeline, and evidence, so your team always has a shared history to work from.
- **Commands** are how you and your members interact with Moddy directly in Discord, from moderation actions to translation and reminders — see the [full command reference](/commands/overview).

Everything above can be configured either from the [web dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard) or with the in-Discord `/config` command, whichever fits your workflow.

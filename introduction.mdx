## What Can Moddy Do?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI AutoMod" icon="robot">
    Automatically detect and act on harmful messages using a multi-stage AI pipeline — no manual rule-tweaking required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Moderation Cases" icon="folder-open">
    Every warning, mute, and ban is tracked as a case with a full timeline, evidence, and appeal support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Social Notifications" icon="bell">
    Follow YouTube channels, Twitch streams, Bluesky accounts, and RSS feeds — post updates automatically in Discord.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inter-Server" icon="link">
    Bridge channels across different Discord servers so your communities can talk to each other in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Welcome Messages" icon="hand-wave">
    Greet new members with fully customizable welcome messages in up to five channels per server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Utilities" icon="wrench">
    Translate text, set reminders, generate timestamps, run OCR on images, roll dice, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
